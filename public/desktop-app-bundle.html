fzInK

fzInK is a free interactive online whiteboard and math workspace for teachers, students, and educators. You can start drawing, writing math formulas, using virtual geometry tools, importing PDF/Word documents, and saving boards locally on your device immediately without signing in.

Optionally, you may connect your Google account to back up and sync whiteboard files created by fzInK to a dedicated fzInK folder in your own Google Drive (using the drive.file scope). fzInK does not generate synthetic/intimate imagery and complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy.

fzInK là ứng dụng bảng trắng tương tác vô tận phục vụ dạy học, ghi chú và toán học, sử dụng miễn phí ngay trên trình duyệt mà không bắt buộc đăng nhập.
