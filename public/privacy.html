Privacy policy

fzInK is an interactive digital whiteboard and mathematics teaching workspace. You can use all core drawing, formula, document import, and local storage features without creating an account or signing in. This policy explains what information fzInK accesses, uses, stores, and deletes when you optionally connect your Google account.

Local-first by default

Drawings and boards stay in your browser IndexedDB or on your device without signing in.

Per-file Drive scope

Access is limited to the fzInK folder and files created or opened by fzInK.

Zero ads or AI training

Google user data is never sold, shared with brokers, or used for profiling or model training.

01

Information we collect and Google user data accessed

When you explicitly choose to sign in with Google to enable optional Google Drive backup and sync, fzInK requests only the following non-sensitive scopes:

  • Basic profile information (openid, email, profile): Your Google account display name, email address, unique user identifier, and profile photo URL are accessed solely to display your signed-in account inside the fzInK interface and identify your active session.
  • Per-file Google Drive access (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file): Access is strictly limited to the dedicated fzInK folder and the specific whiteboard project files (.whiteboard.json) and PNG snapshots (.png) created or opened by fzInK. fzInK cannot read, list, modify, or delete any other files in your Google Drive.
02

How we use your data

  • To display your Google account name, email, and avatar in the workspace header while connected.
  • To create, read, update, rename, and delete your own whiteboard files inside your Google Drive fzInK folder at your request or via the user-controlled auto-save toggle.
  • To maintain your OAuth session securely so you do not have to re-authenticate every 60 minutes.
  • We do not use your Google user data for advertising, analytics profiling, data brokering, or training AI/machine learning models.
03

Data storage, security, and retention

  • Local storage: Your whiteboard drawings, assets, and board library are stored locally on your device in browser IndexedDB.
  • Google Drive storage: Synced whiteboard files and exported PNG images are stored directly in your personal Google Drive account, not on our servers.
  • OAuth session handling: Our Cloudflare Pages Functions (/api/auth/google/*) act only as a stateless OAuth 2.0 relay. Your Google OAuth refresh token is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a server secret and stored either in an HttpOnly; Secure; SameSite=Lax browser cookie (on web) or encrypted on your device via Electron OS-level safeStorage (on desktop). We do not store your whiteboard contents or Google Drive file lists in any external server database.
  • Retention: Session cookies remain until you sign out, revoke access, or the cookie expires (up to 1 year). Files saved in your Google Drive remain there until you delete them.
04

Data sharing and third-party transfers

We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer your personal information or Google user data to any third parties, advertisers, or data brokers. Data is transmitted directly between your browser/app, Google OAuth/Drive APIs, and our stateless Cloudflare Pages OAuth relay solely to provide the Google Drive sync feature you requested.

05

Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

fzInK's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

06

Your rights: revoking access and deleting data

  • You can disconnect your Google account at any time using the Sign out (Đăng xuất) button in fzInK, which clears the encrypted session cookie or local credentials and revokes the OAuth token with Google.
  • You can also revoke fzInK's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To delete saved whiteboard files, delete them directly in fzInK or inside the fzInK folder in your Google Drive, and clear browser storage for local drafts.
07

Contact information

If you have questions about this privacy policy or data handling in fzInK, contact the developer at kiet0905478167@gmail.com.

Chính sách quyền riêng tư — fzInK

Cập nhật lần cuối: 25/11/2026 · Ứng dụng: whiteboardfz.pages.dev

01

Dữ liệu chúng tôi truy cập

Bạn có thể sử dụng toàn bộ tính năng vẽ, công thức toán và lưu bảng cục bộ trên thiết bị mà không cần đăng nhập. Khi bạn chọn kết nối Google Drive, fzInK chỉ xin các quyền không nhạy cảm:

  • openid, email, profile: hiển thị tên, email và ảnh đại diện tài khoản Google của bạn trong ứng dụng.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file: tạo thư mục fzInK trên Google Drive của bạn để lưu, mở, đổi tên hoặc xóa các tệp bảng trắng và ảnh PNG do chính fzInK tạo ra. Ứng dụng không đọc hay can thiệp các tệp khác trên Drive.
02

Cách sử dụng, lưu trữ và bảo vệ dữ liệu

  • Dữ liệu bảng trắng được lưu trên thiết bị của bạn (IndexedDB) và đồng bộ trực tiếp vào Google Drive cá nhân của bạn.
  • Cloudflare Pages Functions chỉ làm cầu nối xác thực OAuth 2.0 không lưu trạng thái; refresh_token được mã hóa AES-256-GCM trong cookie HttpOnly hoặc safeStorage trên máy tính. Chúng tôi không lưu nội dung bảng trắng lên máy chủ bên thứ ba và không bán hay chia sẻ dữ liệu cho quảng cáo hoặc AI.
03

Tuân thủ Google API và quyền thu hồi

fzInK tuân thủ đầy đủ Google API Services User Data Policy (bao gồm Limited Use). Bạn có thể đăng xuất trong app hoặc thu hồi quyền tại myaccount.google.com/permissions. Liên hệ hỗ trợ: kiet0905478167@gmail.com.
